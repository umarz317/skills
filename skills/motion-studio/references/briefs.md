# Creative briefs

Use these as starting points when a user needs a brief. Replace placeholders with supplied inputs and keep the chosen scope. Numerical timings and scene counts are example creative decisions, not universal requirements.

## Product launch

> Create a 20-second launch film for [PRODUCT] using [APPROVED ASSETS]. Communicate [MAIN MESSAGE] to [AUDIENCE], demonstrate verified features, and end with [CTA]. Use [REFERENCE] for visual direction and [MUSIC] for audio. Deliver [FORMATS] with editable source and render instructions.

## Animated explainer

> Explain [TOPIC] to [AUDIENCE] in [DURATION]. Build a clear visual progression from the question to the explanation and takeaway. Use [FACT SOURCES] for claims and [VISUAL REFERENCES] for style. Include [VOICE OR CAPTIONS] and deliver [FORMATS].

## Showreel

> Create a 15-second showreel demonstrating six distinct motion techniques. Establish a coherent visual identity, open with the strongest shot, and synchronize meaningful actions with audio. Deliver the video, source, and a contact sheet.

## Reference-led production

> Analyze [REFERENCE VIDEO OR FOLDER] for composition, palette, typography, transition language, and pacing. Apply those principles to original content about [SUBJECT]. Produce a visual direction and timed shot list, then follow [REQUESTED PLANNING CHECKPOINTS] before animation.

## UI demonstration

> Animate [APPROVED UI STATES] into a coherent product demonstration. Preserve real UI content and brand assets. Make the cursor motivate interactions, keep container and text transitions legible, and synchronize key actions with verified musical cues. Deliver [FORMATS]. Check the loop boundary if a loop is requested.

## Narrative or character-led film

> Create a [DURATION] film about [LOGLINE] for [AUDIENCE]. Use [REFERENCES], [ASSETS], [AUDIO], and [BUDGET]. Establish consistent character and visual direction, make a timed storyboard and representative rough section, then complete the agreed animation and export work.

## Existing-video refinement

> Improve [PACING, LEGIBILITY, AUDIO, OR OTHER ISSUE] in [PROJECT OR VIDEO]. Preserve [APPROVED CONTENT AND VISUAL IDENTITY]. Inspect the relevant timestamps, make focused changes, rerender affected scenes, and compare the final encoded output.

## Critique

> Inspect this video, representative frames, and available audio playback. Report the most consequential defects with timestamps and evidence. Check legibility, composition, pacing, transition continuity, factual accuracy, and audio alignment. State anything you could not inspect. Make revisions only if requested.
